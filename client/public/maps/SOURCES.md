# Radar images

CS2 radar PNGs (1024×1024, composited over black) supplied by the project owner.
They were sourced from CS Demo Manager
(https://github.com/akiver/cs-demo-manager, commit d6c3ac2, `static/images/maps/cs2/radars/`);
see `manifest.json` for the per-file source links. The radar artwork itself
originates from Counter-Strike 2 (Valve). `*_lower.png` files are the lower
layer of multi-level maps and share the main layer's coordinate frame.
